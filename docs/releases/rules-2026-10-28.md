# Rules stand 2026-10-28 (for script 17.30 and later)

**Correction of the PHP2Shell fix builds (CTX697263).** Citrix updated the advisory on 2026-10-27 with the remediation versions: CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 are fixed in **14.1-73.49**, **13.1-64.32**, **14.1-FIPS 73.49** and **13.1-FIPS/NDcPP 37.286**. The rules stands 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-27 carried 73.41 / 64.28 / 37.282, which are the fix builds of the SAML bulletin CTX697191, not of CTX697263; on 2026-10-23 no fixed build for PHP2Shell existed yet. Appliances on 14.1-73.41 to 73.46 and 13.1-64.28 to 64.31 were reported as fixed although they are vulnerable.

**What changes for operators:** with this stand such appliances report `UPDATE` with `vuln63=1`, and block 2 shows management addresses without DENY ACLs as yellow. Until the update, restrict ports 80/443 of the NSIP and of SNIPs with management access to admin networks by ACL (Citrix guidance). Replace `ns_quickcheck.rules` in `/var/nssnapshot/`; no script change needed (17.30 or later).

Details and SHA-256 in `ns_quickcheck.rules.changelog.md`. The test fixtures that represent a fixed appliance moved from 14.1-73.41 to 14.1-73.49.
